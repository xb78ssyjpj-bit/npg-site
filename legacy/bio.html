Where we started

From lecture halls to loading bays.

All three of us met studying music at the University of Manchester. We got our start filming Tiny Desk–style sessions for grassroots Manchester bands — small rooms, one camera, whatever gear we could borrow.

NPG grew out of that, and it still works the same way: the same three people, in the same kind of rooms, working with artists at the stage we started at ourselves.
