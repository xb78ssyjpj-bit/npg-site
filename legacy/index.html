What we're for

A supporting service for artists who are still building.

We aim to support up-and-coming artists around Manchester by taking on the technical and production challenges of high-quality content — so you can get on with the music.

These days you're usually seen on a screen before you're heard in a room. A promoter weighing up a support slot, a curator, a booker in another city — most of them look you up first, and what they tend to find is a phone recording from the back of a venue.

That's the gap. The songs are ready long before the material around them is, and closing that gap is a production job in its own right — the mics, the cameras, the lighting, the mix, the edit. It takes kit most artists don't own and hours most artists don't have spare.

That's the part we take on.

01

The setup

Mics, cameras and a rig chosen around your room and your song — built with you each time, rather than a fixed kit list wheeled in.

02

The technical calls

Levels, sync, lighting, coverage. The decisions that quietly decide whether something sounds professional, and that take years to get right.

03

The finish

Tracked, mixed, cut and graded, and handed over ready to release — in the formats you actually need.

Where we started

A rehearsal room, not a boardroom.

We met studying music at the University of Manchester, and got our start filming Tiny Desk–style sessions for grassroots Manchester bands. The recording and the shooting both grew straight out of that, and we still work the same kind of rooms.
