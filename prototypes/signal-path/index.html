One trunk.
Wherever it needs to come out.

Every job runs the same five stages. What changes is the branch at the end. That is why we are not only a content company, and why you can bring us a problem that isn’t a video.

A room, a song, a date.

You tell us what you’re making and roughly when. You don’t need a kit list, a channel count or a plan. Working that out is the job, not the thing you have to bring to it.

Where it comes out

  • Live record Multitrack capture in the room. Single sessions through to full-length live records, tracked and mixed by people who play.
  • Film Music video, social and short-form, and live session film, built around the track rather than dropped on top of it.
  • Live event Audio and camera production on the day, for shows and events that need both handled by the same people.
  • Dry hire Touring racks and equipment out on their own, for people who already have their own crew.
  • System design The drawing without the rig. We spec the signal flow and the setup; your team runs it.

The gap we close.

These days you’re usually seen on a screen before you’re heard in a room. The songs are ready long before the material around them is, and closing that distance is a production job in its own right.
