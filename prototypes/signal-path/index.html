What we take on.

You don’t need a kit list, a channel count or a plan. Working that out is the job, not the thing you have to bring to it. Tell us what you’re making and roughly when.

  • Live recording

    Multitrack capture in the room. Single sessions through to full-length live records, tracked and mixed by people who play.

  • Film

    Music video, social and short-form, and live session film, built around the track rather than dropped on top of it.

  • Live events

    Audio and camera production on the day, for shows that need both handled by the same people.

  • Touring racks

    Equipment and racks out on dry hire, for people who already have their own crew.

  • System design

    The plan without the rig. We spec the setup and the signal flow; your team runs it.
