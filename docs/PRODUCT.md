## Platform

## Users

Primary visitors are **up-and-coming artists in the Manchester area** — small and independent acts, bands, and ensembles who need high-quality content but do not have the gear, technical knowledge, or time to produce it themselves. Secondary: creative and industry collaborators. Visitors are not the general public; they are people deciding whether to hire or partner with NPG.

Because the audience is artists who are still building, the site must never talk down to them or read as agency-priced. "Up-and-coming" and "still building" are the register; avoid "small artists" as public-facing copy even though it is the internal shorthand.

## Product Purpose

NPG produces quality content for up-and-coming artists, building a **bespoke setup with each artist** so the finished product feels personal to them rather than templated. The site exists to make that mission legible, show what NPG can do, and convert an artist into an enquiry.

## Mission Statement

> We produce quality content for small artists, creating bespoke setups with the artists to provide a final product that feels personal to them.

**Our aim:** to provide a supporting service to up-and-coming artists in the Manchester area by taking on the technical and production challenges of high-quality content.

This is the client's own wording (supplied 2026-07-27) and is the primary mission statement and sales pitch. It outranks earlier framings: the site leads with *why NPG exists* (absorbing the technical burden for artists who are still building), and only then with *what it does* (the two disciplines).

## Positioning

The pitch is a **supporting service**, not a vendor relationship: NPG takes on the technical and production challenges of high-quality content so the artist doesn't have to. Two differentiators carry it:

1. **Bespoke, not packaged** — the setup is built around the individual artist, room, and song, so the output feels personal to them. This is the core claim and must stay central.
2. **Grassroots credibility** — NPG started doing Tiny Desk–style sessions with grassroots Manchester bands, not corporate/agency polish, and works with artists at the stage they're actually at.

Delivery still runs through two lead specialisms — **live recording** (short- and long-form) and **content shoots** (music video, social/short-form, and long-form video content). Social-media content is explicitly in scope and is a major reason artists come to NPG. Touring dry hire and live event audio/camera support remain real, confirmed capabilities available on request, but stay a secondary mention.

## Product Principles

1. **A supporting service, not a supplier** — NPG's purpose is to absorb the technical and production burden for artists who are still building. Lead with that, then with the services.
2. **Bespoke over packaged** — the setup is built with each artist so the result feels personal to them. Never present the offer as fixed tiers or an off-the-shelf package.
3. **Depth over breadth** — live recording and content shoots, done exceptionally well. Everything else is available, not advertised.
4. **Grassroots credibility over corporate polish** — the Manchester local-scene origin is a genuine differentiator, not a footnote.
5. **Small, tight-knit crew** — quality comes from a consistent 3-person team, not scale.

### Claims discipline for the "why quality content matters" pitch
The argument for high-quality content must be made from reasoning an artist recognises, **not from invented evidence**. Do not state platform statistics, algorithm behaviour, engagement percentages, industry survey figures, or claims about what labels/promoters/curators demonstrably do. No numbers unless the client supplies a real, citable source.
